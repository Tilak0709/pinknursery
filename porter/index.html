About this guide

A living part of the workplace

Plants bring natural colour, texture and a sense of calm to shared spaces. This guide introduces the varieties selected for the Porter office and offers practical starting points for their care.

A note on care

Watering needs vary with pot size, light, air-conditioning, season and drainage. Check the potting mix before watering rather than following a rigid calendar. The guidance here is a general indoor-care guide; The Pink Nursery maintenance team adjusts it to the actual conditions in the office.

Dwarf Umbrella Tree

Schefflera arboricola

Also known as Schefflera

A compact foliage shrub with leaflets arranged like the spokes of an umbrella. It is useful for adding a full, leafy form to indoor settings.

Light
Bright to moderate
Water
When the top dries
Native to
Taiwan and Hainan

Care guide

  • Light

    Bright, indirect light; it can tolerate moderate light, though growth may be slower.

  • Water

    Water when the top layer of potting mix dries. Do not keep the roots continuously wet.

  • Care

    Pinch or prune growing tips to maintain a compact shape. Remove dead foliage and check periodically for common indoor plant pests.

Good to know

Each leaf is made of several leaflets radiating from one point, just like the ribs of an umbrella.

Weeping Fig

Ficus benjamina

Also known as Benjamin fig, ficus tree

A graceful indoor tree with slender, arching branches and small, glossy leaves. It brings soft height and a gentle, leafy canopy to corners and reception areas.

Light
Bright, indirect
Water
When the top few cm are dry
Native to
South and Southeast Asia to northern Australia, including India

Care guide

  • Light

    Bright, indirect light, with some gentle morning sun if available. Keep it in one place: moving it often can cause leaf drop.

  • Water

    Water when the top few centimetres of potting mix are dry. Keep it evenly moist but never waterlogged.

  • Care

    Avoid cold drafts and air-conditioning vents. Some leaf drop after a change of position is normal; it usually settles once the plant adjusts. Trim to shape as needed.

Good to know

It is one of the most widely grown indoor trees in the world, and its milky sap is typical of the fig family.

China Doll Plant

Radermachera sinica

Also known as Emerald tree, serpent tree

A compact ornamental tree with delicate, glossy leaflets and a fine-textured canopy. Its lush foliage gives indoor spaces a polished, leafy look.

Light
Bright, indirect
Water
When the top dries
Native to
Southern China and Taiwan

Care guide

  • Light

    Bright, indirect light is best. Avoid sudden changes in position and strong direct sun.

  • Water

    Water when the top layer of the potting mix starts to dry. Do not let the plant sit in excess water.

  • Care

    Keep conditions consistent and avoid frequent moving. Prune lightly to maintain shape and remove dry or damaged leaves.

Good to know

In the wild it grows into a large tree; indoors it stays compact and shrub-like.

Philodendron Moonlight

Philodendron ‘Moonlight’

A compact, self-heading philodendron with bright lime to chartreuse leaves. New leaves open in a glowing yellow-green and deepen gradually, adding a fresh, luminous accent.

Light
Medium to bright indirect
Water
When the top dries
Native to
Cultivated hybrid; philodendrons are from tropical America

Care guide

  • Light

    Medium to bright indirect light keeps the lime colour vivid. Avoid harsh direct sun, which can scorch the leaves.

  • Water

    Water when the top layer of potting mix feels dry. Do not let the pot stand in water.

  • Care

    Use an airy, free-draining mix. Wipe the leaves and remove old lower leaves as they yellow. Keep away from pets, as philodendrons are toxic if chewed.

Good to know

Unlike climbing philodendrons, it grows in a neat rosette from a central crown, so it stays tidy without a support.

Dracaena

Dracaena marginata

Also known as Madagascar dragon tree

A slim, architectural plant with tufts of narrow, red-edged leaves on woody stems. Its upright, sculptural form suits modern interiors and tight corners.

Light
Medium to bright indirect
Water
When the upper half dries
Native to
Madagascar

Care guide

  • Light

    Medium to bright indirect light. It tolerates lower light, but grows more slowly and may lose some colour.

  • Water

    Allow the upper part of the potting mix to dry before watering. It prefers being slightly dry to being overwatered.

  • Care

    Remove dry lower leaves as the stems grow. Brown leaf tips can be trimmed; they are often caused by irregular watering or dry air.

Good to know

As it grows it sheds its lower leaves, leaving the bare, patterned stems that give it a tree-like look.

Money Plant

Epipremnum aureum

Also known as Golden pothos, devil’s ivy

A versatile climbing or trailing foliage plant with attractive green, often golden-variegated leaves. It works well on shelves, partitions and in planters with room for its vines.

Light
Medium to bright indirect
Water
When the top few cm are dry
Native to
Mo’orea, French Polynesia

Care guide

  • Light

    Medium to bright indirect light. Variegated forms generally need brighter indirect light to retain their pattern.

  • Water

    Water when the top few centimetres of potting mix feel dry. Empty excess water from the saucer or planter.

  • Care

    Trim long vines to encourage fuller growth and remove yellow leaves. Provide a support if it is being trained to climb.

Good to know

It is nicknamed devil’s ivy because it is so hard to kill and stays green even in low light.

Song of India

Dracaena reflexa

Also known as Pleomele

A decorative dracaena with narrow, often yellow-edged or variegated leaves arranged along branching stems. It adds colour, structure and vertical interest.

Light
Bright, indirect
Water
When the upper layer dries
Native to
Madagascar and the Indian Ocean islands

Care guide

  • Light

    Bright, indirect light helps maintain leaf colour. Avoid harsh direct sunlight.

  • Water

    Allow the upper layer of potting mix to dry before watering. Avoid constantly wet soil.

  • Care

    Remove dry leaves and prune tips or stems to maintain the desired shape. Use well-draining soil and avoid waterlogging.

Good to know

Its species name, reflexa, means “bent back”, after the way its leaves curve.

Money Plant Climber

Epipremnum aureum

Also known as Golden pothos on a support

The familiar money plant trained upward on a moss stick or climbing support. Grown vertically, it becomes a tall, leafy column of green that suits floor planters and narrow spaces.

Light
Medium to bright indirect
Water
When the top few cm are dry
Native to
Mo’orea, French Polynesia

Care guide

  • Light

    Medium to bright indirect light. Brighter light keeps golden variegation strong.

  • Water

    Water when the top few centimetres of the potting mix are dry. If it climbs a moss stick, mist the stick lightly so aerial roots can grip it.

  • Care

    Tuck new growth onto the support and tie loosely where needed. Trim stems that grow beyond the top to keep the column full.

Good to know

When it climbs, its leaves gradually grow larger than those of the same plant left to trail.

Corn Plant

Dracaena fragrans ‘Massangeana’

Also known as Mass cane

A classic indoor tree with thick cane stems and broad, arching leaves marked with a soft yellow-green central stripe. It adds height with very little fuss.

Light
Medium to bright indirect
Water
When the top half dries
Native to
Tropical Africa

Care guide

  • Light

    Medium to bright indirect light. It tolerates lower light, though the central stripe may fade.

  • Water

    Allow the top part of the potting mix to dry before watering. Reduce watering when growth slows.

  • Care

Good to know

Its species name, fragrans, refers to its sweetly scented flowers, which open at night on mature plants.

Lady Palm

Rhapis excelsa

Also known as Broadleaf lady palm

An elegant, clumping palm with fan-shaped leaves held on slender, fibre-covered canes. It brings a refined, dense, upright form to lobbies and workspaces.

Light
Low to bright indirect
Water
When the top dries
Native to
Southern China and Taiwan

Care guide

  • Light

    Bright, indirect light is ideal, but it copes well with lower light. Protect it from strong direct sun.

  • Water

    Water when the top layer of the potting mix starts to dry. Keep evenly moist but not waterlogged.

  • Care

    Remove old or brown leaves at the base. Wipe the leaves occasionally and avoid using hard water or excess fertiliser, which can brown the tips.

Good to know

New canes keep sprouting from the base, so the clump slowly thickens year after year.

Lucky Bamboo

Dracaena sanderiana

Also known as Ribbon plant

Sculptural stalks with fresh green leaves, often grown in tiers or bundles. A popular symbol of good fortune that suits desks and reception counters.

Light
Medium, indirect
Water
Keep roots covered
Native to
Central Africa

Care guide

  • Light

    Medium to bright indirect light. Avoid direct sun, which can scorch the leaves.

  • Water

    If grown in water, keep the roots covered and change the water every one to two weeks. If grown in soil, keep it lightly moist.

  • Care

    Use filtered or stored tap water where possible, as chlorine and salts can brown the leaf tips. Rinse the container when changing the water.

Good to know

Despite its name, it is not a bamboo at all but a dracaena.

Also known as Golden umbrella tree

An umbrella tree with glossy leaflets splashed in gold and green. It makes a bright, cheerful feature plant for open office areas.

Light
Bright, indirect
Water
When the top dries
Native to
Umbrella trees come from East Asia and Australasia

Care guide

  • Light

    Bright, indirect light keeps the golden variegation strong. Avoid harsh afternoon sun.

  • Water

    Water when the top layer of the potting mix is dry. Do not keep the roots continuously wet.

Areca Palm

Dypsis lutescens

Also known as Butterfly palm, golden cane palm

A popular clumping palm with arching, feathery fronds. Its soft silhouette adds height, movement and a welcoming tropical character to an office.

Light
Bright, indirect
Water
When the top dries
Native to
Madagascar

Care guide

  • Light

    Bright, indirect light is ideal. Some gentle morning sun may be tolerated after gradual acclimatisation.

  • Water

    Water when the top layer of soil begins to dry, keeping the mix lightly moist but not soggy.

  • Care

    Ensure good drainage, wipe dust from fronds and remove fully brown leaves. Avoid prolonged dry conditions and cold drafts.

Its stems turn golden-yellow as they mature, which is why it is also called the golden cane palm.

Dracaena Striped

Dracaena fragrans ‘Warneckii’

Also known as Striped dracaena

An upright dracaena with long, sword-shaped leaves finely striped in green, grey and white. Its crisp, graphic foliage suits contemporary interiors.

Light
Low to bright indirect
Water
When the top half dries
Native to
Tropical Africa

Care guide

  • Light

    Low to bright indirect light. It is one of the more shade-tolerant dracaenas.

  • Water

Snake Plant

Dracaena trifasciata

Also known as Mother-in-law’s tongue

A hardy, architectural foliage plant with upright, sword-shaped leaves. Its neat growth habit makes it a popular choice for contemporary offices and indoor spaces.

Light
Low to bright indirect
Water
When fully dry
Native to
Tropical West Africa

Care guide

  • Light

    Low to bright indirect light; growth is usually better in brighter indirect light.

  • Water

    Allow the potting mix to dry out fully or almost fully before watering. Avoid leaving the pot standing in water.

  • Care

    Use a free-draining potting mix. Remove dry leaves at the base and wipe dust from the leaves. Overwatering is a common cause of problems.

Good to know

It uses CAM photosynthesis: its leaf pores open at night to take in carbon dioxide, which helps it save water.

ZZ Plant

Zamioculcas zamiifolia

Also known as Zanzibar gem

A tough, glossy foliage plant with upright stems of neat, waxy leaflets. It looks polished all year and is one of the easiest plants for offices.

Light
Low to bright indirect
Water
When fully dry
Native to
Eastern Africa

Care guide

  • Light

    Low to bright indirect light. It grows faster and fuller in brighter indirect light.

  • Water

    Let the potting mix dry out completely before watering. Overwatering is the main cause of problems.

  • Care

    Wipe the leaves to keep their shine. It needs little else: occasional feeding and repotting only when the roots fill the pot. Keep away from pets.

Good to know

It stores water in thick underground rhizomes, which is why it can go weeks without watering.

Calathea

Goeppertia (formerly Calathea)

Also known as Prayer plant family

A decorative foliage plant prized for its patterned, painted-looking leaves. It adds rich colour and texture to desks, shelves and low planters.

Light
Medium, indirect
Water
Keep lightly moist
Native to
Tropical America

Care guide

  • Light

    Medium, indirect light. Direct sun fades and scorches the patterned leaves.

  • Water

    Keep the potting mix lightly and evenly moist. Do not let it dry out completely or sit in water.

  • Care

    It likes humidity: group it with other plants and keep it away from air-conditioning vents. Use filtered or stored water if leaf edges brown.

Good to know

Its leaves rise and fold upward in the evening and open again in the morning, a daily movement typical of the prayer plant family.

Monstera

Monstera deliciosa

Also known as Swiss cheese plant

A bold tropical plant with large, glossy leaves that develop natural splits and holes. It brings an instantly lush, statement look to open spaces.

Light
Bright, indirect
Water
When the top few cm are dry
Native to
Southern Mexico to Panama

Care guide

  • Light

    Bright, indirect light encourages larger, split leaves. Avoid strong direct afternoon sun.

  • Water

    Water when the top few centimetres of the potting mix are dry. Reduce watering in cooler months.

  • Care

    Wipe the large leaves regularly and provide a moss stick or stake as it grows. Guide aerial roots into the pot or onto the support. Keep away from pets.

Good to know

Young leaves are usually whole; the characteristic splits and holes appear as the plant matures.

Dumb Cane

Dieffenbachia seguine

Also known as Dieffenbachia

A leafy, upright plant with large leaves splashed in cream and green. It brings a bright, generous, tropical look to indoor spaces.

Light
Medium to bright indirect
Water
When the top dries
Native to
Tropical America

Care guide

  • Light

    Medium to bright indirect light. Rotate the pot so it grows evenly toward the light.

  • Water

    Water when the top layer of the potting mix is dry. Avoid waterlogged soil.

  • Care

    Wear gloves when pruning and wash hands afterwards. Keep it out of reach of children and pets, as the sap irritates the mouth and skin.

Good to know

Its common name comes from its sap, which can make the mouth and throat swell so that speaking becomes difficult for a while.

Bird of Paradise

Strelitzia species

A striking statement plant recognised for its large, paddle-shaped leaves. It creates a bold, sculptural focal point in spacious office areas.

Light
Bright, some gentle sun
Water
When the upper soil dries
Native to
Southern Africa

Care guide

  • Light

    Bright light is important; a position with some gentle direct sunlight can support strong growth.

  • Water

    Water thoroughly when the upper portion of the potting mix has started to dry. Reduce watering in cooler, lower-light periods.

  • Care

    Give leaves room to spread, wipe them gently and remove old or damaged leaves. Rotate the pot occasionally for balanced growth.

Good to know

It is named after its flowers, which look like the head of a brightly coloured bird.

Rubber Plant

Ficus elastica

Also known as Rubber fig, Indian rubber tree

A bold foliage plant with large, thick, glossy leaves. Its strong upright form makes it an elegant feature plant for office interiors.

Light
Bright, indirect
Water
When the top few cm are dry
Native to
Northeast India and Southeast Asia

Care guide

  • Light

    Bright, indirect light. Acclimatise gradually if moving it to a brighter position.

  • Water

    Water when the top few centimetres of soil are dry. Reduce frequency when light levels or temperatures are lower.

Cast Iron Plant

Aspidistra elatior

Also known as Bar-room plant

An almost indestructible foliage plant with tall, dark green, glossy leaves rising straight from the soil. It thrives in dim corners where other plants struggle.

Light
Low to medium indirect
Water
When the top half dries
Native to
Southern Japan

Care guide

  • Light

    Low to medium indirect light. Direct sun can bleach and scorch the leaves.

  • Water

    Allow the upper part of the potting mix to dry before watering. It tolerates occasional neglect better than overwatering.

  • Care

It earned its name by surviving the dim, smoky rooms of the 19th century, where few other plants lasted.

Anthurium

Anthurium andraeanum

Also known as Flamingo flower

Light
Bright, indirect
